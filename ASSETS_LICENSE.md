## Character / model assets (`public/models/`, GLB)

Known tree includes third-party character packs commonly used in demos (e.g. **Mixamo Xbot / Soldier** style rigs).

| Asset class | Likely source | Status |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Humanoid GLB (Xbot / Soldier / similar) | Mixamo / community sample packs | **Attribution residual** — confirm original download terms before redistribution |
| Generated or bundled props | Project-created or CC0 samples | Prefer CC0 / project-owned |

## What we do not claim

- We do **not** invent a clean license for every GLB in the tree.
